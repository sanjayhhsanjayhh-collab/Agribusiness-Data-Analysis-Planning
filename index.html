Interpretation & data limitations

Read before using any result for a decision.

Research integrity

Source and coverage. The supplied Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (DA&FW) table contains selected All-India crop/category statistics for 2021–22 to 2025–26. The last year is marked as the third advance estimate in the source. Units are lakh hectares for area, lakh tonnes for most production, lakh bales for cotton production (1 bale = 170 kg), and kg/ha for yield.

Aggregation. “Total Food Grains” and “Total Oilseeds” overlap with individual crop rows and should not be added to them. Category charts exclude those aggregates. Production comparisons also exclude cotton when units would be incompatible.

What is not present. The dataset does not contain farm-gate or retail prices, transport costs, post-harvest losses, weather series, input costs, or state-level detail. Therefore the dashboard does not claim observed price spreads, supply-chain losses, or causal climate effects.

Forecast caution. Five annual observations are too few for strong predictive conclusions. The final year is used as a one-observation holdout solely to demonstrate the method. Forecasts are methodological examples—not official estimates or investment/farming advice.
